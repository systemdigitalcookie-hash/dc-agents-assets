# COSEC Meeting Papers (demo)

A clickable sample of a board-meeting papers portal for Eco World Development Group Berhad. One HTML file. No build, no backend, no sign-in.

Data stays in this browser (`localStorage`). **Reset demo data** in the amber banner puts the sample pack back.

## Open

After this folder is on `main`, GitHub Pages serves it at:

https://systemdigitalcookie-hash.github.io/dc-agents-assets/play/bp-ewdg-7k3m/

You can also open `index.html` directly.

Due dates are shown as at **2 Dec 2026**, so the December Board pack stays “due today”.

## Three ways to view it

Use the switcher at the top.

- **COSEC** creates each submission link, reviews the file, and publishes it to directors.
- **Department** opens the link, uploads once, and gets a receipt. The file is then locked. A new version needs a new link from COSEC, with a reason and what changed.
- **Director** reads published papers only, for the Board and the committees they sit on, dated on or after their appointment. The Resource Centre has no date limit. A resigned director has no access.

Try Low Mei Ling, Ng Soon Lai, Nor Rejina binti Abdul Rahim, and Dato' Leong Kok Wah.

## Sample data

Director names, roles, and appointment dates are from the public FY2025 Integrated Annual Report and are shown as sample data. Meetings, papers, uploads, and the department contact names are made-up. Finance, Sustainability, and Call Centre are the departments used in the story. Departments marked Example are not an official organisation chart.

File names follow the meeting-paper style, for example `ACM 2026.2027 (01.11.2026-31.10.2027)` and `ACM 01 (04.11.2026) - 03.0 Internal audit function assessment FY2026 v1.0.pdf`.
